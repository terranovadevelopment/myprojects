About Me — Willis Butts

I've been a programmer since 1980, with decades of experience in systems design and systems engineering. I approach each project as a complete system: understanding the problem, designing a practical solution, and building software that works for the people who use it.

My work and interests include custom programming, web development, online platforms, and community-focused technology. I enjoy finding straightforward solutions to complex problems.

Available for custom programming and systems-design projects — rates starting at $30 per hour.
